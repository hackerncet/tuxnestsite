GETTING STARTED

Simple answers before you sign in

How do I get a student account?

Authorized BCA faculty enroll your USN. Use the temporary credentials given to you privately and change your password at first sign-in.

Who can see an issue I raise?

Your private request is available to you and the authorized BCA HOD. It is not part of the student directory or public search results.

Will my personal contact details appear online?

No. Students can choose to publish their portfolio, but the public directory does not display mobile numbers or email addresses. Review certificate links before sharing them.
